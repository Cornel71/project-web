01 · about

I keep clusters calm and deploys boring.

I'm a DevOps / Platform Engineer focused on building infrastructure that teams can trust: Kubernetes platforms that don't page anyone at 3am, GitOps pipelines that make "what's deployed" a question with one obvious answer, and automation that removes toil instead of relocating it.

My day to day lives across container orchestration, Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD design and observability — with a habit of writing things down so the next engineer (often future me) doesn't have to guess.
